How to Play

Goal

  • Move left and right to dodge incoming enemy shots.
  • Shoot enemies before they reach the bottom of the screen.
  • Defeat the boss at the end of each level to unlock the next one.
  • Collect power-ups for shields, extra lives, rapid fire, and explosive bullets.

Controls

  • Move: Left / Right arrows or A / D (touch: ◀ / ▶)
  • Shoot: Hold Space or touch FIRE
  • Pause: Esc
  • Upgrade: Use the Upgrades button in the HUD
